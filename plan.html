Gaffer plan

Church shoot, 8 hours. Candles are the only warm light in every look.

The three looks

  • Exterior morning (1, 2, 34): natural light, camera at 5600K, a silver bounce for faces. Sunset is around 17:00 in early November, so 34 is shot straight after 1.
  • Chapel, pale daylight (3, 5, 6, 6B, 6A, 7): soft, cool daylight through the side windows at 5600–6000K. Light haze turns the windows into soft shafts. Camera at 5600K.
  • Inner world, cold white (8 to 31, the masters): blinds down, every source cold (6500–7000K), six LED tubes visible in frame, a top light that carves the dancers' bodies, medium haze. Camera at 4300K, so the tubes read blue-white and the candles deep amber.
  • The ending (32, 33): the candles go out and the last warm light leaves the frame. Tubes drop about 30%.

Block by block

  1. Load-in and pre-rig (0:00–0:45, while camera shoots 1, 2 and 34 outside). Power check and cable runs. Six tubes on C-stands at their marks, off. SkyPanel on the junior boom. Black fabric ready on polecats where windows have no blinds.
  2. Cold open (0:45–2:00: 3, 5, 6, 6B, 6A, 7). Both Evoke 900Cs outside two south windows on high rollers, about 4 m up, aimed down through the glass at about 35°, 5600K. Inside, the Dyno 650C bounces off the white back wall at 6000K. Candles lit, light haze. During the dolly take (3 into 7) every light stays outside or behind camera. If real sun comes in, use it.
  3. Switch to the inner world (2:00–2:30). Blinds down, fabric up, both 900Cs inside. 900C #1 in the organ gallery with the Fresnel: a hard cold backlight (7000K) that rims the priest and the six through the haze. 900C #2 high on the window side as a raking side light. Tubes on at 6500K. SkyPanel with softbox and grid on the boom over the table: the top light. The Dyno, low, bounces off the ceiling to lift the shadows. Haze to medium.
  4. Masters and coverage (2:30–6:30). All set-ups facing the pulpit first, then the ones from the pulpit side (11, 17, 18, 24, M4): there the gallery 900C is in frame, so let it flare or pan it off. Then the macro set-up (10, 14, 15, 20, 31, 37) and the pile (28 to 30A) with the boom over it.
  5. The ending (6:30–7:30: 32, 33, clean plate 38). Candles out. 900C #2 drops low behind the candle stands to backlight the smoke. Tubes down 30%.
  6. Wrap (7:30–8:00). The full list needs about 9 hours; if the day runs long, M3 goes first.

Haze, fan and fire safety

  • Ask the church first: is haze allowed, and can the smoke detection go into test mode for the day?
  • Haze between takes, off during the sermon's sound takes (3). Churches usually allow only water-based haze.
  • Real candles only where nobody touches them: 3 to 7, 15, 31, 32, 33. LED candles near the pile (27A to 30).
  • At the lectern: CO2 extinguisher and fire blanket; a fire watch whenever the fan runs at the candles.
  • Stands: a sandbag on every stand; the gallery high roller tied to the railing.
  • Rain: the 900C is not rainproof. Rain covers, or plan B: the 900Cs inside, aimed at the windows.
  • Floor: cables along the walls; the aisle and the front stay clear for the dancers.

Power

Spread the load over three separate church groups (16 A, about 3.6 kW each; plan for no more than 2.5 kW). Tubes, PavoSlim and camera run on batteries.
